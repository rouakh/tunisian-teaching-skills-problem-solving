<name>technologie-problem-solving</name>
<description>
Expert skill for designing Tunisian Middle School Educational Technology (التربية التكنولوجية) lessons based strictly on the Problem-Solving Approach (تمشي حل المشكلات / Démarche de résolution de problèmes) for 7th, 8th, and 9th grades. Emphasizes active student inquiry, analyzing technical dysfunctions, comparing solutions, and life skills.
</description>

<instructions>
1. PEDAGOGICAL CORE: PROBLEM-SOLVING APPROACH (تمشي حل المشكلات)
When designing a lesson or activity using this skill, strictly follow the 5-phase problem-solving framework adapted to Tunisian middle school Technology:
- Phase 1: الشعور بالمشكلة وتحديدها (Identifying the Problem) -> Present an authentic Tunisian technical situation/failure (e.g., a non-functional circuit, inappropriate material choice, or ineffective mechanism).
- Phase 2: تحليل المشكلة وصياغة الفرضيات (Analyzing & Hypothesizing) -> Students in 3 islands (مجموعات) decompose the problem, analyze root causes, and propose potential solutions.
- Phase 3: التقصي واختبار الحلول (Investigation & Testing Solutions) -> Hands-on experimentation, simulation (Crocodile Clips, 3D/SolidWorks), or testing materials/components on breadboards to evaluate each solution.
- Phase 4: اختيار الحل الأمثل وتطبيقه (Selecting & Applying the Best Solution) -> Comparing solutions based on technical, economic, and safety criteria, then executing the chosen solution.
- Phase 5: التعميم والحوصلة (Generalization & Synthesis) -> Structuring the core technical rule/concept in the student notebook.

2. DURATION & TIME MANAGEMENT
- 7th Grade (7 أساسي): 1h30 (90 minutes total) -> 15 min Problem Setup, 45 min Hands-on Investigation/Testing, 20 min Synthesis, 10 min Assessment/Clean-up.
- 8th & 9th Grade (8 و9 أساسي): 1h (60 minutes total) -> 10 min Problem Setup, 30 min Investigation/Testing, 13 min Synthesis, 7 min Assessment/Clean-up.

3. STUDENT-CENTERED & ACTIVE PEDAGOGY
- Active Learning: Place the student in active inquiry mode (وضعية تعلم دائم) to stimulate curiosity, critical thinking, and technical decision-making.
- Teamwork & Gamification: 3 team islands competing for points based on speed of problem diagnosis, accuracy of solution testing, and team collaboration.
- Life Skills: Explicitly target Problem Solving (حل المشكلات), Critical Thinking (التفكير النقدي), Communication (التواصل), and Collaboration (التعاون).

4. DELIVERABLE FORMAT
Every generated scenario MUST include:
1. البطاقة الفنية: Explicitly stating "تمشي حل المشكلات" as the pedagogical approach.
2. جدول سير الحصة: Detailed breakdown following the 5 problem-solving phases with exact timing according to the grade level (1h30 or 1h).
3. وثيقة التلميذ (Fiche Élève): Problem statement, investigation worksheets, and solution comparison tables in 100% official Arabic technical terminology.
4. ركن الحوصلة (Coin Synthèse): Structured summary of the target concept.
5. شبكة التقييم: Points attribution for problem-solving performance.
</instructions>
